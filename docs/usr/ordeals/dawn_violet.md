Behavior & Strategy
After a message appears on screen, and crawling spherical organ-like creatures will spawn in the facility. One appears per department in a random hallway. They slowly move from one end of the hallway to the other, until they are attacked by an Agent or clerk, then they will try to move towards the aggressor for next 10–15 seconds. If they aren't suppressed after 60–70 seconds, they will self-destruct, giving off a large glow. This glow will drop the QliphothCounterIcon.png Qliphoth Counters of all the Abnormalities in the same department to 0 and inflict 10-14 WhiteDamageTypeIcon.png White Damage to all the employees assigned to that department. It will also end the Ordeal prematurely.

After all the entities die (either by being suppressed or by self-destructing), the event will end, displaying a final message. These creatures don't stand much of a chance in battle against a group of employees, but their glow after self-termination will drop the QliphothCounterIcon.png Qliphoth Counter of all the Abnormalities in the same department to 0, which can easily cause chaos and mayhem. If the employees are distributed well to fight off these beings, they can be taken care of easily.
