Behavior & Strategy
After a message appears on the screen, brown worm-like creatures with teeth at the front will appear in groups of 5 in one hallway per Department. When a target is in the room, they will leap towards it, dealing 1-2 RedDamageTypeIcon.png RED Damage per hit. The worms will remain in the hallway for 40–60 seconds, and will burrow into the ground and move to another random hallway in the facility. When the worms sprout up from the ground, they will deal 1-2 RedDamageTypeIcon.png RED Damage to those in the room.

Since the worms move in groups and move erratically, they are a threat against weak employees, particularly those with either Level 1 Fortitude or a Suit weak to Red Damage. Due to their movement while attacking, the worms can quickly switch to the opposite side of the target, making melee weapons and slow-attacking weapons harder to hit with if the employee's Attack Speed is too low. Hence, it is highly suggested to use Ranged weapons or fast-attacking weapons if possible. If an employee is low on health, make sure to take note they will be slowed when walking through the worms, so send them out early before their health gets too low.

After defeating all the worms, the event will end, displaying a final message.
