Standard Training-Dummy Rabbit

## Basic Information
0-00-00	TETH

Standard Training-Dummy Rabbit (0-00-00) is a Risk Teth.png TETH Class Abnormality that is capable of escape.

## Ability
Its special ability activates when its QliphothCounterIcon.png Qliphoth Counter reaches 0. It will decrease by 1 when Repression.png Repression is used on it.

When the counter is depleted, the Abnormality will breach but will remain immobile outside the Containment Unit's door. It possesses low HPIcon.png HP at 100 and doesn't have any other special abilities. It is easy to suppress and will flip upside down when defeated.

## Details
WorkSpeedIcon.png Base E-Box Speed is 0.70 boxes per second (14.3s total work time at base), and QliphothMeltdownIcon.png Work Cooldown is 10s.

When breaching, HPIcon.png Max HP is 100, and MovementSpeedIcon.png Speed is 5.0, it does not have any attacks.
