One Sin and Hundreds of Good Deeds

## Basic Information
O-03-03	ZAYIN

One Sin and Hundreds of Good Deeds (O-03-03) is a Risk Zayin.png ZAYIN Class Abnormality. One Sin and Hundreds of Good Deeds will always be the first Abnormality the player gets.

## Ability
Its special ability restores 4 points of SP of the agent who was working on it if the Work Result is Good. If the employee collects all the 10 E-Boxes, One Sin will heal 4 points of SP to all Agents belonging to the department.

## Details
Base E-Box Speed is 0.30 boxes per second (33.3s total work time at base), and Work Cooldown is 10s. This applies to all Abnormality by default.
